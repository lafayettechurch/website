# Editing the website

This guide is for the people who keep lafayettechurch.org up to date. You don't need to know any code.

## Signing in

1. Go to **lafayettechurch.org/studio**.
2. Sign in with Google or your email. (Ask the site admin to invite you if you don't have access yet.)

## Making a change

1. On the left, choose **Church details** (times, address, office hours, links) or a page.
2. Find the box you want to change and type. Your changes save as a draft automatically.
3. Click **Publish** at the bottom right.

The live site updates within a minute. Refresh the page on the website to check.

Changed your mind before publishing? Use the menu next to Publish and choose **Discard changes**.

## The yellow [bracketed] tags

Anything in square brackets, like `[Office to write: where to park]`, shows on the website as a small yellow tag. It means "this still needs writing." Those fields also show a yellow warning in the editor. Replace the whole thing, **including the brackets**, with the real text.

## Photos

- Kyle's photo: **Leadership → Minister → Photo**. Portrait (taller than wide) works best.
- Shepherd couples: **Leadership → Shepherds → Shepherd couples**, then click a couple.
- Drag a photo in, or click **Upload**. Then click the crop icon and drag the circle onto the faces so the photo crops around them, and click **Publish**.
- Until a photo is added, a sand-colored "Photo" frame shows in its place.

## The calendar

Events come straight from the church's Google Calendar. Add or change them there, and the website catches up within about 15 minutes. Events marked **Private** in Google Calendar never appear on the website.

## Writing tips

- Talk to "you". Keep it warm, plain and short.
- Sentence case for headings: "Plan your visit", not "Plan Your Visit".
- Write times as "10 AM" and "7 PM".
- Explain church words the first time: "Shepherds (our elders)".
- No emoji, and go easy on exclamation points.

## If something goes wrong

Every page has a history (the clock icon at the top of the page), so you can see and restore recent versions. The site admin also keeps nightly backups. Email them and say which page and roughly when, and they can put it back.
