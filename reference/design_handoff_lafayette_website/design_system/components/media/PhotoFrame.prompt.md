Photo-ready frame: a sand panel with a facet accent and label until a real photo is dropped in via `src`.

```jsx
<PhotoFrame ratio="4:5" label="Photo of Kyle" />
<PhotoFrame ratio="16:9" src="img/building.jpg" alt="Our building on New Ballwin Road" />
```

- Fills its container width; the ratio sets height. 4px radius, no border, no shadow.
